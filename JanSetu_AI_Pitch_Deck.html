THE PROBLEM

The 3 Compounding Barriers to Welfare & Civic Rights

⚖️

Bureaucratic Jargon & Legal Obscurity

  • Official gazette circulars and bylaws are written in dense statutory language for officials, not citizens.
  • People struggle to tell whether an income, turnover or age threshold applies to them.
  • Rules sit across central, state and municipal sources, with no single plain-language view.
🗣️
10.6%

Regional Linguistic Exclusion

  • Only about 10.6% of Indians reported speaking English, so English-first portals exclude most citizens.
  • Literacy was 74.04%: about 1 in 4 people cannot be served by text alone.
  • Lack of spoken-word access also locks out visually impaired beneficiaries.
🏦

Last-Mile Administrative Leakage & DBT Stalls

  • An eligible citizen’s DBT payment can still bounce if Aadhaar is not mapped to the bank account on the NPCI mapper.
  • Only banks can change that mapping, and many citizens never learn this is the problem.
  • Middlemen exploit the information gap by charging illegal “approval fees”.
Sources: Census of India 2011 (English speakers across first, second and third language; effective literacy rate); NPCI Aadhaar Payments Bridge SOP.2 / 8

AI Reasoning & the 3-Stage Agent Pipeline

Stage 1

Statutory Taxonomy Agent

  • Classifies type: scheme, notification, bylaw, policy
  • Extracts the administering ministry, category, official portal and named authority
  • Reads closing dates with the source words that state them
→
Stage 2

Dynamic Rule Graph Tool

  • Turns clauses into interactive rule objects, not static bullet points
  • Income and age limits become live numeric checks
  • Extracts ₹ amounts and isolates disqualification clauses
→
Stage 3

Reflection & Multilingual Guardrail

  • Checks amounts, rule quotes, dates, helplines and emails directly against the source text
  • Removes what it cannot find; rejects incomplete cards
  • Five-language card and spoken brief

EXCLUSIVE PRODUCT INNOVATIONS

What Sets JanSetu Apart

“Do I Qualify?” Dynamic Rule Simulator

  • Renders legal clauses as live iOS-style switches and income or age inputs.
  • Computes an instant Citizen Readiness Score (0–100%) and lists what is still missing.

Apple Control Center Voice Engine

  • Browser voices by default, with optional Gemini voices: English male or female, regional female.
  • Playback scrubber, speed switching (0.75x–1.5x) and a live highlighted transcript.

Public Comment & Objection Dispatch

  • Generates a structured, editable representation letter for draft policies, citing their proposed clauses.
  • Copy it, or open it in email when a verified address is in the source.

Cryptographic SHA-256 Citizen Receipts

  • Generates JS-SHA256-… reference IDs with the browser’s Web Crypto API.
  • Tied to the document, the time and the readiness score; works with no connectivity.
All four run in the browser, with no server of our own.5 / 8
